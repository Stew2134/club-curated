Brand Takeovers

Club Culture are excited to collaborate with a variety off exciting promoters and brands within the electronic music scene, on our broadcast platform. Providing a perfect opportunity for both live DJ sets and recorded content.

How do brand takeovers work?

A takeover gives a brand the opportunity to programme and host its own curated lineup within the studio for a dedicated session, typically lasting between three and six hours. The full session is streamed via the Club Culture radio platform, and participating DJs and brands are provided with recordings of their sets afterwards, allowing them to download and share the mixes across their own social media channels.

Promotion & Artwork

Brands are encouraged to create and lead their own artwork and promotional rollout ahead of the stream, allowing full creative control over the direction and identity of the takeover. We also support promotion by sharing content across Club Curated’s social channels.

Although please note, we have a zero tolerance policy towards the use of AI-generated artwork.

Programming + Inclusivity

We allow brands to programme their takeovers freely; however, we expect thoughtful representation of diverse communities within the lineup, ensuring inclusive and balanced programming across all sessions.

Rates & Availability

We aim to offer flexibility across brand takeover bookings, which are typically scheduled on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays. Sessions are offered at a discounted rate of £10 per hour (minimum of three hours) to help cover studio costs. Full payment is required in advance to secure your booking.

Logistics and General Information

We can host up to four guests in our studio at any given time. If your group is larger, there are wonderful local businesses nearby, like the pub next door, (The New Oxford) that can make your visit even more enjoyable.

To ensure a creative, professional, and welcoming space for all, brand bookings must adhere to our studio rules and terms and conditions.

Studio Policies and Terms and Conditions

The Club Curated Studio is a professional working space and community hub. We ask that all visitors adhere to the following terms to help us maintain a welcoming environment and support our mission of providing a creative, professional, and enjoyable space for everyone.

Before booking and/or attending a session with ourselves, please familiarise yourself with the below:

By booking or attending a session at Club Curated Studio, you agree to these Terms & Conditions on behalf of yourself and any guests. The lead booker is responsible for ensuring that all members of their group are aware of and comply with these guidelines.

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

If at any point during your booking these guidelines are breached, we reserve the right to cancel your session immediately without refund.

Arrival Times & Lateness

Please arrive on time for your booked session.

If you arrive late, unfortunately we cannot extend your booking time, and sessions cannot run beyond the allocated slot. Lateness does not qualify for a refund or partial refund.

Smoking & Vaping

Smoking or vaping is strictly prohibited inside the studio.

If the fire alarm is triggered due to smoking or vaping, the Fire Brigade may issue an £800 emergency call-out fee, which will be charged to the lead booker and must be paid within 7 days of notification. The lead booker is also responsible for the actions of any guests.

Guests are welcome to step outside for a smoke break under the guidance of studio staff.

Equipment & Studio Care

All studio equipment must be treated with care and respect.

Any damage caused by misuse, negligence, or inappropriate handling will result in the lead booker being responsible for full repair or replacement costs.

Do not touch speakers, wiring racks, or installed cabling under any circumstances. The studio space, including décor and furnishings, should also be respected.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Cancellations made at least 48 hours in advance will receive a voucher to rebook another session.

Last-minute cancellations cannot be refunded, but you may transfer your session to a friend or contact us for assistance filling the slot.

Consideration of the Building & Neighbours

The studio is part of a shared building with other studios and professional musicians.

Please respect the surrounding spaces by avoiding excessive noise, shouting, or disruptive behaviour.

While guests are welcome to enjoy themselves, the studio must be treated as a professional working environment and is not a designated party venue.

Guests

For radio bookings, we allow one guest per visitor.

For practice sessions, we allow up to three guests, whose names must be provided at the time of the initial booking.

If you arrive with additional guests beyond the number specified at booking, we can only provide entry to the confirmed guests. Any remaining guests will be asked to wait outside. There’s a great pub next door for anyone waiting!

If you wish to add a guest after booking, please contact us in advance at studio@clubcurated.co.uk. Approval will depend on capacity and availability.

Alcohol Consumption

Alcohol consumption is permitted within the studio, however the studio is not a designated party venue.

If any guest appears intoxicated or behaves in a manner that breaches these terms, we reserve the right to cancel the session immediately. The lead booker is responsible for the conduct of all guests.

Cleaning & Litter

All guests are asked to remove any litter or rubbish from the studio at the end of their session to help maintain a clean and welcoming environment.

If a substantial mess is left behind, the studio reserves the right to charge a cleaning fee to the lead booker.

CCTV & Privacy

CCTV is in operation within the studio for the safety and security of clients and equipment.

All recordings are handled in accordance with GDPR regulations.

Liability

Club Curated and its team members are not liable for injuries, accidents, or personal property loss/damage within the studio space or building.

Visitors use the space at their own risk.

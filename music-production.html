About

Teaming up with KTMX Studio's, Club Curated are excited to share their dedicated music production studio - based within Bexley Square. A dedicated space, created by artists, for artists, catered for Electronic Music.

 

The cozy, fully treated studio is perfect for producing, mixing, and mastering your music. Chill on our big comfy sofa surrounded by a warm, inspiring vibe, or get creative with our collection of analogue synths, Ableton Push, guitars, basses, MIDI keyboards (including a fully weighted with 88 keys!) , studio monitors, and pro mics. All you need is your laptop, and creativity.

Book your session here!

Music Production Studio Hire Information

You can book the hours you need and arrive up to 15 minutes early to set up before your session. We kindly ask that you begin packing up 5 minutes before the end of your booking to ensure a smooth transition for the next session.

We provide industry-standard professional equipment, so all you need to bring is your laptop - we’ve got the rest covered. You’re always welcome to bring your own instruments or any specific gear you’d like to use during your session.

Yes, we welcome up to two guests per session due to studio space limitations. If you need to bring additional guests or musicians, please contact us in advance, and we’ll work with you to find a suitable solution.

We offer a wide range of equipment, including synthesizers, professional microphones, studio monitors, MIDI keyboards (ranging from fully weighted 88-key controllers to compact 25-key options), a variety of guitars and basses, Ableton Push, and more. For a detailed equipment list, please visit our booking page.

Yes, you’re absolutely welcome to vlog, film, or bring your own photographer as needed. We encourage you to do so and would love for you to tag us or share your experience on social media.

Please arrive 15 minutes before your session. We’ll welcome you into the waiting area, and around five minutes before your slot, we’ll take you upstairs to the studio so you can get settled before the start of your set.

Once your session is booked, you’ll receive an email with a contact number.

When you arrive at Bexley Square (you’ll know you’re there when you see the metal horse!), text the number — we’ll come down to greet you and let you in.

If you’re running late, please text us as soon as possible. Unfortunately we are not able to extend your allocated slot, due to following bookings.

If you cancel at least 48 hours in advance, we’ll issue a voucher so you can rebook.

If you can’t make it last minute, you’re welcome to transfer your slot to a friend or ask us to help fill it.
